# About the Khôlles

I give weekly oral mathematics examinations (khôlles) at Lycée Hoche in Versailles. Since this role belongs to the French CPGE system, here is what it involves.

CPGE is an intensive two-year undergraduate program in mathematics and physics, preparing students for nationwide competitive entrance exams to top French engineering schools. Throughout the program, students sit weekly oral mock exams that both prepare them for the entrance exams and check their grasp of the course.

I examine 6 students each week, preparing course questions and exercises tailored to the students I am sitting with. A few examples are below.
